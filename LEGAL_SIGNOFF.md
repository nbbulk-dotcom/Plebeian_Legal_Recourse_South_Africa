# LEGAL SIGNOFF REQUIREMENTS

## Constitutional Liberation Platform - Legal Governance Framework

### MANDATORY LEGAL REVIEW PROCESS

All document templates in the Constitutional Liberation Platform MUST be reviewed and approved by qualified legal practitioners before production deployment. This ensures constitutional compliance, legal accuracy, and user protection.

### LEGAL REVIEWER QUALIFICATIONS

Legal reviewers must meet ALL of the following requirements:

1. **Admitted Attorney**: Must be an admitted attorney of the High Court of South Africa
2. **Law Society Registration**: Current registration with provincial Law Society
3. **Minimum Experience**: Minimum 5 years post-admission experience
4. **Specialization**: Relevant specialization in constitutional law, property law, or civil procedure
5. **Good Standing**: No disciplinary action or suspension from Law Society
6. **Constitutional Knowledge**: Demonstrated expertise in South African constitutional law

### APPROVAL PROCESS

1. **Template Submission**: Developer submits template for legal review
2. **Reviewer Assignment**: Qualified legal reviewer assigned based on specialization
3. **Legal Analysis**: Comprehensive review of:
   - Constitutional compliance
   - Statutory compliance
   - Procedural compliance
   - Risk assessment
   - User protection measures
4. **Approval Decision**: 
   - ✅ **APPROVED**: Template approved for production use
   - ⚠️ **REQUIRES REVISION**: Template needs modifications
   - ❌ **REJECTED**: Template cannot be approved
5. **Documentation**: All decisions documented with detailed reasoning

### PRODUCTION DEPLOYMENT RESTRICTIONS

🚨 **CRITICAL SAFETY MEASURE**: No document template may be deployed to production or made available to users until it receives formal legal approval from a qualified reviewer.

### LEGAL DISCLAIMERS

All AI-generated documents must include appropriate legal disclaimers based on approval status:

#### For APPROVED Templates:
```
LEGAL NOTICE: This document has been reviewed by qualified legal practitioners for constitutional and statutory compliance. However, this does not constitute legal advice. Users should consult with an attorney for specific legal guidance.
```

#### For PENDING/UNAPPROVED Templates:
```
⚠️ DEVELOPMENT VERSION - NOT FOR LEGAL USE
This document template has NOT been approved by legal practitioners and is for development/testing purposes only. DO NOT use for actual legal proceedings.
```
